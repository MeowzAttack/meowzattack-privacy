Privacy Policy

MeowzAttack Video Uploader

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how MeowzAttack Video Uploader handles information when you connect a Pinterest account and publish Pins. The uploader is a local application that runs on your computer.

Information the app handles

When you use the Pinterest connection, the app receives basic Pinterest account information and reads account or board information needed to show your available destinations. If you choose to publish, the app sends the video, Pin title and description, and selected board or publishing options to Pinterest.

The app may also process video thumbnails and other upload details you provide. It sends content only to the platform destinations you select.

Where information is stored

Pinterest authorization tokens are stored on your computer using Windows Credential Manager. Upload history and staged videos or thumbnails are stored in the app's local data folder. Completed staged media is removed after all selected destinations finish successfully. Media for failed or unfinished uploads may remain so you can retry; the app also cleans up unneeded staged files after 48 hours. You can remove upload-history entries in the app.

How information is shared

The app does not operate a MeowzAttack cloud service for your uploads. Information is sent to Pinterest when you connect your account or publish a Pin. If you choose another supported platform in the app, the content and details needed for that upload are sent to that platform. Those services handle information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

Information kept by the app remains on your computer unless you choose to upload it to a platform. Protect access to your Windows account and computer. Pinterest and any other selected platform may retain published content under their own policies and settings.

Your choices

You choose whether to connect Pinterest and which content to publish. You can disconnect the account in the app and revoke the app's access from your Pinterest account settings. You can delete local upload-history entries in the app; staged media for failed or unfinished uploads can remain locally until cleanup or removal.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data handling changes. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
